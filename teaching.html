🪐 Teaching & Outreach

🌟 Astronomy Education & Olympiads

My teaching journey began with the Bangladesh Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (BDOAA), where I have served as a mentor, problem author, and organizing committee member. The BDOAA’s mission is to inspire high school and college students across Bangladesh to engage with the cosmos through problem-solving and collaborative learning.

I trained and mentored students who went on to represent Bangladesh at the International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (IOAA). In 2022, I served as the Team Leader for Bangladesh at the IOAA held in Kutaisi, Georgia.

Learn more about our national astronomy initiative at bdoaa.org/team.

⚛️ Quantum Computing Education

In addition to astronomy, I am passionate about quantum computing education and the democratization of emerging technologies. As one of the founding members of The Wond’ry Quantum Studioat Vanderbilt University, I helped create a nationwide network of students and researchers dedicated to advancing quantum literacy.

At the Quantum Studio, I co-developed an open-access curriculum that introduced students and professionals to quantum computing, quantum algorithms, and quantum machine learning. I also led workshops that translated complex quantum mechanics concepts into intuitive, visual learning experiences.
