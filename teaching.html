Teaching & Outreach

Throughout my academic journey, I have been passionate about sharing knowledge and inspiring others. My teaching philosophy centers on fostering curiosity, critical thinking, and inclusivity in the classroom and beyond.

  • Led and mentored students in international Olympiads, helping them develop problem-solving skills and a love for astronomy.
  • Organized and participated in educational initiatives to make science accessible and engaging for diverse audiences.
  • Developed open-access resources and guides, such as the Quantum Computing Education Guide, to support learners at all levels.

I look forward to continuing to teach, mentor, and inspire the next generation of scientists.
