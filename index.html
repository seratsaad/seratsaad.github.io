Hello, I’m Serat Saad 🌌

I am a Ph.D. student in Astronomy at The Ohio State University, where I study wide binary stars and their dynamics in the context of galactic structure, planet engulfment, and Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND).

I earned my bachelor’s degree from Vanderbilt University, where I double-majored in Physics and Mathematics and minored in Astronomy and Scientific Computing. At Vanderbilt, I worked on research projects involving young stellar objects using BOSS data from SDSS-V, and later worked on active galactic nuclei and supermassive black hole binary spectroscopy.

Beyond research, I’m passionate about science communication, Astronomy Olympiad, and problem solving.
